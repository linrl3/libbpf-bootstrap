# Translation / 翻译

## Original English Text

As Phase 1 of the painting of the Condominium is nearing completion, we ask that you check your individual areas, i.e. balcony & windows for any current paint splatter & if you have any, please inform, with photos, the MA Office. Please have this done no later than 10 Jan 26. Please note: Once the painting is completed & all has been signed off, it will be very difficult if near impossible, to get the painters back to rectify these issues.

## Chinese Translation / 中文翻译

公寓第一期油漆工程即将完工，请您检查各自的区域，即阳台和窗户是否有油漆溅污。如有发现，请附上照片通知管理处（MA Office）。请务必在2026年1月10日前完成检查并反馈。请注意：一旦油漆工程全部完成并签署验收后，要让油漆工人返回处理这些问题将会非常困难，甚至几乎不可能。

**注：** 原文中的 "10 Jan 26" 理解为2026年1月10日。
